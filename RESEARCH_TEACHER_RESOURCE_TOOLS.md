# Teacher resource tools: focused comparison

Research date: 26 October 2026. Status: research and opportunity hypotheses, not an approved product or implementation plan. Sources below are first-party pages inspected during this review; no product was installed or tested.

Current implementation decisions are in [PROJECT_MEMORY.md](PROJECT_MEMORY.md), [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) and [DEMO.md](DEMO.md). Earlier offline-first and discovery recommendations below are research history, not additional implementation gates. CherIt's current website-offline/editor functionality is deferred; local downloaded files remain part of the demo.

## Finding

A general repository of curriculum resources and ready-made lessons would substantially overlap existing official services. Offline access and curriculum curation also already exist in Kolibri. A defensible opportunity would need teacher validation of a narrower preparation workflow, such as selecting a specific BOW target and obtaining a small, source-labelled, classroom-ready pack that remains usable offline. This is a hypothesis, not evidence that existing tools lack it.

## Curriculum and content maintenance implications

- The LS guide publishes an explicit update date, while BLD Hub describes lesson-planning implementation guidance for Term 2. These pages do not establish that the curriculum universally changes once per year or that a yearly refresh is sufficient. Record curriculum/BOW version, grade, learning area, term/week where applicable, source and verification date. [LS guide](https://sites.google.com/deped.gov.ph/lsguide), [BLD Hub](https://sites.google.com/deped.gov.ph/bldhub/teach)
- The official LS lesson-planning page labels its exemplar collection as soon to be ILAW aligned for Term 2, 2026. Existing availability does not establish every file is already aligned to the newest format. Project GRASP also links official BOW and grade-grouped exemplars. A curriculum's phased rollout across school years must not be equated with universal annual curriculum replacement. [Official lesson-planning guide](https://sites.google.com/deped.gov.ph/lsguide/lesson-planning), [Project GRASP](https://sites.google.com/deped.gov.ph/projectgrasp/revised-k-to-10-curriculum)
- DepEd LR Portal terms require attribution, noncommercial use and share-alike conditions, and warn that other permissions may matter. Resource records also publish conditions of use. Check each chosen asset before redistribution or adaptation; do not assume everything under an official link is unrestricted. Preserve source links and resource-level permissions. This is a product-content requirement, not a legal clearance. [Portal terms](https://lrmds.deped.gov.ph/terms), [sample record](https://lrmds.deped.gov.ph/detail/26407)
- Offline delivery still needs an initial installer/content transfer and a way to distribute later upgrades. Kolibri explicitly documents that distinction. A claim of automatic synchronization while disconnected would be misleading. [Kolibri FAQ](https://kolibri.readthedocs.io/en/latest/faq.html)
- Official DepEd Commons help remains online but highlights 2021 announcements. Its current production availability and parity with current services were not established, so do not present it as a verified current competitor or claim it was discontinued. [Official help](https://sites.google.com/deped.gov.ph/icts-usd/helpdesk/deped-commons)

## Opportunity hypotheses to validate with the interviewed teacher

1. Finding materials is not the complete job: teachers may still need to verify alignment, assemble a usable sequence, adapt for available classroom materials, and keep it accessible offline. Ask for the last actual example and existing files.
2. A small teacher-reviewed pack for one grade, subject and BOW target may be more useful than another large catalogue. Compare it against the same task using the official portal and BLD Hub.
3. Reliability may mean showing exactly which source supports a competency and flagging old packs for review. Automatic collection does not establish instructional alignment or official approval.

## Earlier discovery recommendations

Confirm the interviewee's grade/subject and locality; obtain their actual applicable CG/BOW and recent lesson-plan example; observe how they find and prepare one lesson today; test official alternatives with them; establish their offline device/storage/printing needs; identify who would curate updates. Measure preparation time and successful offline retrieval in a small task comparison, without promising any saving beforehand.

Not checked: authenticated workflows, exhaustive tool coverage, every source asset's rights, or adoption demand beyond the user's reported interview. No application, installation, framework, service configuration, commit or push was created.

## Literacy evidence check, 26 October 2026

- PSA's 2025 Eastern Visayas Regional Social and Economic Trends, Table 10.1, reproduces the national 2024 FLEMMS regional table: Bicol functional literacy is 67.0%, compared with 70.8% nationally. This is a 3.8 percentage-point difference. The regional figures were verified in the search-indexed text of the PSA-hosted PDF; the full PDF exceeded the browser tool's size limit and a direct download returned 403. [PSA table](https://rsso08.psa.gov.ph/system/files/publication/2025%20Regional%20Social%20and%20Economic%20Trends%20-%20Eastern%20Visayas.pdf)
- The measure covers people aged 10–64, not only currently enrolled pupils. Functional literacy includes reading, writing, computation and comprehension, including integrating information and making inferences. It is distinct from basic literacy and from attainment of a particular school grade's learning competencies. [PSA national release, 4 April 2025](https://psa.gov.ph/content/every-10-filipinos-9-have-basic-literacy-while-7-have-functional-literacy?vcode=32)
- PSA states that the 2024 FLEMMS adopted revised operational definitions and methodology. Do not interpret a simple difference from earlier survey rates as a comparable decline. The regional estimate does not establish the cause of learning gaps, prove that teachers' preparation burden caused the literacy result, identify each learner's skill level, or demonstrate that this proposed product will improve literacy. Use it as context for why appropriate learning support matters, then validate the product through teacher tasks and classroom-suitable materials. [PSA methodology statement](https://psa.gov.ph/content/every-10-filipinos-9-have-basic-literacy-while-7-have-functional-literacy?vcode=32)
